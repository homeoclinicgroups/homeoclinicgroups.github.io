About Us

Fahad Homeo Clinic has been providing gentle, natural homeopathic care to families across Karachi through our branches. Founded by Dr. Muhammad Hashim, whose teachings and time-tested formulas continue to guide our practice, the clinic is now led by Dr. Fariha Sultana. We focus on treating the root cause of health concerns rather than just the symptoms, using homeopathic remedies tailored to each patient's individual needs. Our goal is simple: safe, personalized care you can trust, close to home.
